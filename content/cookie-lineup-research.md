# Cookie Lineup Research

Detailed supply chain breakdown for each of the 9 Girl Scout cookie varieties — every cookie's main ingredients, where each comes from, and the distinctive supply chain story.

**Common to ALL Girl Scout cookies (and shared across the lineup):**
- **Palm oil** — RSPO Mass Balance, sourced from Indonesia & Malaysia (~9,500 mi by ship to Long Beach)
- **Sugar** — US sugar beets (top states: Minnesota, North Dakota, Idaho, Michigan)
- **Wheat flour** — Hard Red Winter wheat, primarily Kansas and the Great Plains
- **Soybean oil** — Illinois (#1, 688M bu in 2024), Iowa, Indiana
- **Salt** — US (Louisiana 34% / Texas 17% / New York 15% / Kansas 7% / Utah 6%)
- **Leavening** (baking soda) — US chemical industry
- Baked at **ABC Bakers** (North Sioux City, SD) or **Little Brownie Bakers** (Louisville, KY)
- Shipped in **100% recycled paperboard boxes**

## 1. Thin Mints (flagship)

**Distinctive:** chocolate-coated mint cookie. The most international supply chain in the lineup.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | RSPO Mass Balance certified since 2022; ~9,500 mi by ship |
| Cocoa | Ivory Coast & Ghana (West Africa) | ~70% world supply; ~6,500 mi by ship to East Coast |
| Sugar | US sugar beets (MN, ND, ID, MI) | ~95% are GE varieties |
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains (Kansas #1) | Hard Red Winter wheat |
| Peppermint oil | US Pacific NW (Idaho 40%, Oregon 30%, Washington 23%) | Pacific NW = 93% of US peppermint oil |
| Soybean oil | US Midwest (Illinois, Iowa, Indiana) | |

**Country count:** ~6 (Indonesia/Malaysia, Ivory Coast/Ghana, USA, plus minor)
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.46–0.8 kg CO₂/box (literature 1.81 kg CO₂eq/kg × 0.255 kg box)
**Story:** Olivia Chaffin's discovery of "mixed" RSPO Mass Balance palm oil started a national conversation about food supply chain transparency.

## 2. Samoas / Caramel deLites

**Distinctive:** chocolate-and-caramel ring cookie covered in toasted coconut. Adds a Southeast Asian coconut leg on top of Thin Mint's supply chain.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Coconut (toasted)** | **Philippines / Indonesia / India** | These three countries grow ~72% of world coconut. 2024 prices spiked 73% due to El Niño droughts |
| Cocoa | Ivory Coast & Ghana | Same as Thin Mints |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| Corn syrup | US Midwest (Iowa #1 — 2.6B bu corn in 2024) | For caramel and sweetener |
| Condensed milk | US dairy (California is #1 dairy state, makes 51.6M lbs of butter — over 7× the next state) | For caramel |
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |

**Country count:** ~7
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.9 kg CO₂/box (highest of the bunch tied with Exploremores — multiple international + dairy chain)
**Story:** Coconut adds a labor angle similar to palm oil — Indonesia and Philippines have documented worker safety issues on plantations. Coconut prices have been volatile due to climate (El Niño droughts).

## 3. Tagalongs / Peanut Butter Patties

**Distinctive:** peanut butter sandwich dipped in chocolate. A *transatlantic* combination — US peanuts meet West African cocoa.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Peanuts** | **US South — Georgia produces ~42% of all US peanuts** | Plus AL, FL, NC, TX. ~1.8M acres in 2024 |
| Cocoa | Ivory Coast & Ghana | Same chocolate coating as Thin Mints |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |
| Soybean oil | US Midwest | |

**Country count:** ~5 (US peanuts replace some of Thin Mint's overseas ingredients, but cocoa still adds a transatlantic leg)
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.85 kg CO₂/box
**Story:** Peanuts are a Southern US story — Georgia outproduces all other peanut states combined. Combined with West African cocoa, a Tagalong literally crosses the Atlantic to meet at the bakery.

## 4. Do-si-dos / Peanut Butter Sandwich

**Distinctive:** *the most domestic cookie in the lineup* — no chocolate coating, no significant overseas ingredients except palm oil.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Peanuts** | US South (Georgia 42%) | Same as Tagalongs |
| **Oats** | **US Upper Midwest — Minnesota leads, with ND, SD, WI, IA** | Top 4 = >50% of US oat production |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains | |
| Corn syrup | US Midwest (Iowa, Illinois) | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |

**Country count:** ~4 (palm oil is the only major international ingredient)
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.7 kg CO₂/box (one of the lowest — short supply chain)
**Story:** A Do-si-do is mostly a road trip across the American Midwest and South — Iowa corn meets Minnesota oats meets Georgia peanuts meets Kansas wheat. The palm oil is the only ingredient that crosses an ocean.

---

## 5. Trefoils / Shortbread

**Distinctive:** the *simplest* cookie. Buttery shortbread shaped like the trefoil logo. Almost everything is domestic.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains (Kansas) | The dominant ingredient by mass |
| **Butter** | **US dairy — California is #1 (51.6M lbs/yr), then Pennsylvania, Wisconsin** | What gives shortbread its rich flavor |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | The only international ingredient |
| Salt | US (Louisiana, Texas) | |

**Country count:** ~3–4 (palm oil is the only major international ingredient)
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.6 kg CO₂/box (lowest in the lineup — shortest supply chain)
**Story:** Trefoils are the cookie that's *almost* a 100-mile diet. If Girl Scouts ever swapped palm oil for US butter or sunflower oil, this would be the closest to a fully domestic cookie. The shape is also the official Girl Scout trefoil — the most iconic in the lineup.

## 6. Lemon-Ups / Lemonades

**Distinctive:** crisp lemon cookie. Lemon flavoring is the only ingredient with a potentially European supply chain.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains | |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| **Lemon oil / lemon flavoring** | **Italy (Sicily — 86% of Italian lemons), Spain, or US (California, Florida)** | Cookie-makers don't disclose specific source. Italian/Spanish oils traditionally considered highest quality |
| Eggs | US (Iowa is #1 egg producer, plus OH, IN, PA) | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |
| Salt | US | |

**Country count:** ~5 (lemon oil could add Italy or stay domestic)
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.65 kg CO₂/box
**Story:** Lemon oil is one of the most globally-traded flavor ingredients. Sicilian lemon oils have been produced for over 130 years and are valued for high citral content. Whether Girl Scout lemon flavoring crosses the Atlantic is genuinely unclear from public info.

## 7. Toffee-tastic (gluten-free)

**Distinctive:** the only gluten-free cookie. Replaces wheat with a multi-grain blend that has its own diverse supply chain.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Rice flour** | **US (Arkansas, California, Louisiana, Missouri)** | Arkansas grows ~50% of US rice |
| **Tapioca starch** | **Brazil, Thailand, Indonesia** | From cassava root — tropical, imported |
| **Sorghum flour** | US (Kansas, Texas) | Kansas is #1 for sorghum |
| Potato starch | US, Canada, or imported | |
| Butter | US dairy (California) | |
| Brown sugar | US sugar beets/cane | |
| Toffee bits | Made from sugar + butter (US) | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |

**Country count:** ~5 (the tapioca adds a tropical layer; everything else mostly US)
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.7 kg CO₂/box
**Story:** The gluten-free flour blend is its own mini supply chain — US rice from Arkansas, US sorghum from Kansas, plus *imported tapioca starch from Brazil or Southeast Asia*. The cookie that seems "simple" is actually pulling from four continents.

---

## 8. Adventurefuls

**Distinctive:** brownie cookie with caramel center and sea salt — the *most chocolate-forward* cookie in the lineup.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cocoa** | Ivory Coast & Ghana | Heavier cocoa content than Thin Mints since the cookie *is* a brownie |
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains | |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| Brown sugar | US | For caramel and brownie depth |
| **Butter** | **US dairy (California)** | For the caramel center |
| Corn syrup | US Midwest (Iowa) | For caramel |
| **Sea salt** | US — Great Salt Lake (Utah) or San Francisco Bay (California) for solar-evaporated sea salt | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |

**Country count:** ~6
**Carbon estimate:** ~0.85 kg CO₂/box
**Story:** Adventurefuls debuted in the 2022 cookie season and was the first new flavor in five years. The sea salt topping comes from US solar evaporation operations (Utah's Great Salt Lake is one of the world's largest sea salt producers).

---

## 9. Exploremores

**Distinctive:** rocky-road-inspired sandwich cookie — adds California almonds and a marshmallow story to the lineup. Brand new for 2026.

| Ingredient | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Almonds (toasted)** | **California — 80%+ of world's almond supply, 100% of US almonds** | 1.3M acres of orchards in CA. ~1.1 gallons of water per single almond — water-intensive |
| Cocoa | Ivory Coast & Ghana | Sandwich coating |
| Wheat flour | US Great Plains | |
| Sugar | US sugar beets | |
| **Marshmallow crème** | Made of: corn syrup (US Iowa), sugar (US), gelatin (US — animal byproduct from beef/pork industry) | |
| Brown sugar | US | |
| Palm oil | Indonesia / Malaysia | |

**Country count:** ~7
**Carbon estimate:** ~1.0 kg CO₂/box (one of the heaviest — almonds + cocoa + multi-source marshmallow)
**Story:** The newest cookie (debuted 2026 season, announced September 9, 2025). The California almond story is environmentally complex — almonds are nutritious but water-intensive in a drought-prone state. Marshmallow gelatin is a byproduct of the US meat industry, which is itself one of the highest-carbon supply chains on the lineup.
