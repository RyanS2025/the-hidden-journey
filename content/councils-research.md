# Council-to-Bakery Mapping (Research Draft)

Mapping of all Girl Scout councils to their bakery (ABC Bakers in North Sioux City, SD or Little Brownie Bakers in Louisville, KY), plus draft HQ cities for the council calculator's shipping-distance feature.

**Sources:**
- **Little Brownie Bakers "Our Councils"** page — direct, authoritative LBB list, [littlebrowniebakers.com/our-story/our-council](https://www.littlebrowniebakers.com/our-story/our-council) (accessed 2026-05-03)
- **Wikipedia "List of councils (GSUSA)"** — comprehensive list of all 112 councils, [wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_councils_(Girl_Scouts_of_the_USA))
- ABC Bakers councils derived by subtraction (everything not on LBB's list)

**HQ City confidence labels:**
- ✓ Confident (city is in the council name, or council covers a single major metro)
- ~ Likely (state's largest city / known regional hub)
- ? Verify (multiple plausible HQs — spot-check on the council's own website)

**⚠️ Caveats:**
- Council assignments can change year-to-year. Example: **Girl Scouts of Utah switched LBB → ABC** for the 2024 season.
- HQ cities labeled `~` or `?` should be spot-checked by visiting the council's "Contact Us" page.
- For the calculator, exact HQ matters less than the regional ballpark — Ryan's distance calculation via Google Maps will give correct shipping miles to either bakery once the city is locked in.

## Next research step: distance to bakery

Once the HQ city for each council is locked in, Ryan can wire up Google Maps API (or a similar service) at build time to compute:

- Distance from council HQ → ABC Bakers (North Sioux City, SD) for ABC councils
- Distance from council HQ → Little Brownie Bakers (Louisville, KY) for LBB councils

This number (the "domestic shipping leg") is what the council calculator multiplies against truck-emissions per mile to show how the carbon footprint varies by location.

For the JSON format (`data/councils.json`), each entry will look like:

```json
{
  "name": "Girl Scouts of Greater Los Angeles",
  "states": ["CA"],
  "bakery": "LBB",
  "hq_city": "Los Angeles, CA",
  "distance_to_bakery_mi": null
}
```

`distance_to_bakery_mi` stays `null` until the build script runs.
